# Quality gates and delivery

1. Completeness: actual full PPTX files exist for all requested variants; their slide and note counts match the plan. No contact sheet slices masquerading as slides.
2. Scientific validity: verify every displayed statistic, unit, data split, comparator and confidence interval against a source locator. Separate actual results from simulation/teaching examples or future intentions. No unsupported universal claims.
3. Asset integrity: original evidence is not recolored/relabelled; source figure crops preserve axes/legends and main context. Generated objects do not claim experimental validity.
4. Embedded media: inspect PPTX zip relationships to confirm slide images point to packaged media; folder existence does not count. Record missing/misreferenced images.
5. Layout: render all slides, inspect contact sheets and individually inspect pages with dense plots or equations. Correct clipping, extreme font shrinking, overlap, inconsistent spacing, broken images, orphan labels and unreadable axes.
6. Typography: verify declared fonts, display font availability, and that any font-substitution claim is supported. Embedded bitmap text is not changed by editable text font assignment.
7. Spoken flow: read all notes from first to last and check continuity, figure reading sequence, timing and factual consistency.
8. Templates: if original master/logo/header was requested, verify actual source assets and stable relative alignment. A recreated similar header is not identical unless demonstrated.
9. Variant audit: identical facts, data and sources across variants, with meaningfully different design choices. Run the same full QA for each.
10. Report honestly: distinct labels for automated structural checks, rendered inspection, actual Microsoft PowerPoint testing, and ImageGen/tool availability. Do not claim unperformed checks.

11. Content-slide composition: visually estimate text-box area against the 20–40% target, inspect for text-only pages and isolated numbers that could be combined with relevant evidence or context, and revise where appropriate. Record justified exceptions and any unavailable image-generation capability. Do not describe this visual estimate as an automated measurement.

## Deliverables
For each version: editable PPTX, PDF and montage when supported, notes, and material list. Shared: narrative outline, evidence ledger, generated asset files, mapping of images to slides and sources, variant difference note, and QA report. If a deliverable fails, explain exactly what is missing.
